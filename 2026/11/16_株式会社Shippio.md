Thank you for using Shippio.

We sincerely apologize for the significant inconvenience and concern caused by the temporary suspension of our service and the breach of personal information and other data resulting from the unauthorized access to Shippio Platform, which we discovered on Monday, November 2, 2026.

Following our 

First Report (published on Wednesday, November 4: 第一報のリンクは省略)
Second Report (published on Thursday, November 5: 第二報のリンクは省略)

We are providing an update on the progress of our investigation, our findings, and the status of our reports and notifications, as set out below.

## 1. Information Confirmed as Acquired (as of 16:00 JST, Friday, November 13)

This notice covers only the changes and additions since the First and Second Reports. For all other details, please refer to those reports. The specific scope of impact varies by customer. As of today, our investigation has found no misuse of personal information and no other secondary harm resulting from this incident.

Newly Identified Matters

Billing-related information:The Second Report stated that billing-related information was among the affected data. Our subsequent detailed log analysis has confirmed that the amounts billed to customers were not acquired. However, we have confirmed that billing details for some shipments (such as charge item names, quantities, and our costs) were acquired.

## 2. Notification to Customers

Since Thursday, November 5, our representatives have been individually contacting customers whose document file contents were acquired.We have also begun notifying, on a rolling basis, customers whose personal information was acquired through the unauthorized access to the platform.

## 3. Recurrence Prevention and Reporting to External Organizations

We have submitted a preliminary report to the Personal Information Protection Commission. We are also consulting with an external cybersecurity specialist organization regarding a detailed root cause investigation and a further review of the scope of impact, and we are working on measures to prevent recurrence.

## 4. Requests to Customers

We kindly ask you to confirm the following points and take the necessary actions once again.

- Reset your password and login again

We have reset the login sessions and passwords of all users. Please make sure you have reset your password at least once since the resume of our service on last Thursday, 5th Nov. You can do so using the "Forgot your password?" link on the login screen. If you use your Shippio password for other services as well, we also strongly recommend changing it there.

- Verify your data and beware of suspicious communications

Checking against originals: Before using any data or CSV files you downloaded before the service suspension, or any data imported via API, please compare it against your original records.
Suspicious files and communications: If you find any unfamiliar document files attached to a shipment, or if you receive a suspicious email or call from someone claiming to be a Shippio employee (for example, asking for your password or a verification code), please do not open any links or files, and contact us at the address below.

## 5. Going Forward

We will continue to work toward a full understanding of the incident through the investigation with external specialist organization, notify the affected customers and individuals, and strengthen our security measures to prevent recurrence. We will announce on this page promptly once the investigation and reporting are complete, or if any new facts come to light.

We take this serious incident with the utmost gravity and will do everything we can, as a company, to prevent recurrence and to regain your trust.

Once again, we sincerely apologize for the considerable inconvenience and concern this has caused.
